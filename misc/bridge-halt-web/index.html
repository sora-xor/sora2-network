SORA bridge emergency halt

Guardians use this page to stop the SORA bridge workers during an attack. The halt is written on chain and costs guardians no fee. The chain rejects it from any other account, so for them the button does nothing. Every bridge node stops its workers when it sees the halt, and devops restart them on their servers. No Root call is needed.

        What a halt does

        • Writes a numbered halt on chain. The HASHI offchain worker on every bridge node stops as soon as it sees the halt: it signs no withdrawals and imports no deposits.
        • The workers stay stopped until devops restart each node with --bridge-resume-after-halt and the halt's number. No Root call or vote is needed, and guardians cannot restart anything.
        • The relayers for the EVM, TON and Liberland bridges do not read the halt yet. Devops stop those relayers on their servers.
        • Does not make the chain refuse bridge calls: transfers sent during a halt wait for the workers. Root's recovery latch still does that when governance sets it.
        • Does not pause contracts on Ethereum, TON or Liberland. Withdrawals already signed by the bridge can still be redeemed there; pause those contracts separately.
        • Has no effect from accounts that are not guardians: the chain rejects the transaction before it reaches a block, so nothing is written and no fee is charged.
        • Within a minute of a halt the chain refuses another one, so guardians who react together send a single halt.
